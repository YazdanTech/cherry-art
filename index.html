About the artist

Latifa AlNuaimi

Daughter of a poet

My Story

Emirati artist whose practice combines abstract calligraphy, painting and sculpture.

“I paint the way a poem is read, slowly, then all at once. Each mark begins as a word I could not say, and ends as a shape you can feel.”

Latifa AlNuaimi is an Emirati artist based in the UAE. Her practice brings together abstract calligraphy, painting and sculpture, drawing on the expressive possibilities of Arabic letterforms.

Inspired by her father, the poet Rabee bin Yaqoot Alnuaimi, she approaches poetry as a source of imagery, feeling and artistic exploration, a conversation between language, gesture and visual rhythm.

Her abstract world gathers playful lines, soulful shapes and unexpected colour, spirals, moons and cherries in cream, red, blue and black.
